Case of the Day — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game “Case of the Day” (the “Game”) handles, why, and what choices you have. We have tried to keep it short and plain. The Game has no accounts, no login and no chat.

1. Who is responsible (controller)

The controller of your personal data is Maria Kugacz (Rainy Evening Games), a private individual based in Poland (European Union). Contact: frameoneproduction@gmail.com.

2. What data is processed

  • Game progress (solved cases, coins, office furniture, daily streak, settings). Stored only locally on your device. We do not receive it. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.
  • Advertising data (Google AdMob): advertising ID, device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version), IP address and the approximate location derived from it, ad interaction data (e.g. that a rewarded video was watched), and diagnostic data.
  • Purchase data (Google Play Billing): when you buy a hint pack or subscription, payment is processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details; we only receive a confirmation that the purchase is valid, so the Game can deliver the item.
  • Feedback (optional): if you choose to send the in-game feedback form, we receive your rating, the text you type, the app version, the language of the Game, a random installation ID and the numbers of that case (case number, result, stars, mistakes, hints used, time). Please do not include personal details in the text. Nothing is sent unless you press “Send”. Feedback is saved in a Google Sheet on the developer's Google Drive (via Google Apps Script).
  • Game statistics (only if you tap “Yes, help” in the Game; you can switch it off at any time in Settings): game events such as “case opened” or “case solved”, a random installation ID (not your advertising ID), the day number since installation, the app version, language and platform. The data we store does not include your name, email, advertising ID or IP address. We use no analytics SDK: events are sent to a Google Sheet on the developer's Google Drive (via Google Apps Script). Until you agree, nothing is sent.
  • Notifications: the daily “new case is ready” reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device. No data is sent to us for it. You can turn it off in the Game or in your phone settings.
  • Web version (if you play in a browser at our GitHub Pages address): the game itself runs entirely in your browser and keeps your progress in the browser's local storage on your device. We use no cookies, no advertising and no analytics there. Like any website, GitHub (the hosting provider) may process technical data such as your IP address in its server logs, under its own privacy statement.

3. Why we use it and on what legal basis (GDPR)

  • Running the Game and delivering purchases — performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Personalised ads — your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)). If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised ads, which use limited data for things like frequency capping, fraud prevention and measurement, based on our legitimate interest in funding a free game (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Feedback — to read and answer your feedback and improve the Game; legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)), triggered by your own action of sending it.
  • Game statistics — your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), asked once after your first case; you can withdraw it at any time in Settings.
  • Legal obligations such as tax and accounting records of sales (Art. 6(1)(c)).

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Consent for ads in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the Game shows a Google-certified consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are used. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the Game under Settings > Privacy. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before.

5. Third parties

We use the following services from Google (Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA/Switzerland; Google LLC elsewhere). They process data according to their own terms:

  • Google AdMob — advertising. How Google uses data from apps that use its services
  • Google Play / Google Play Billing — distribution and payments.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Feedback and game statistics are stored in a Google Sheet on the developer's Google Drive and received with Google Apps Script. Google acts as our processor for this. Some providers, including Google, may transfer data outside the EEA; such transfers are protected by safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

6. How long we keep data

  • Game progress: on your device until you delete it or uninstall the Game.
  • Feedback and game statistics: up to 14 months in the Google Sheet, then automatically deleted (earlier if you ask us to).
  • Advertising and purchase data: kept by Google according to its own retention periods; purchase records needed for tax purposes are kept for as long as the law requires.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data deleted (“right to be forgotten”);
  • restrict or object to processing, including processing based on legitimate interest;
  • data portability;
  • withdraw consent at any time (for ads: Settings > Privacy in the Game);
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl. You may also contact the authority in your own EU country.

To use your rights, write to frameoneproduction@gmail.com. Because the Game has no accounts, we may ask for details that help us find your data (for example, the approximate date and text of your feedback). You can also reset your advertising ID in your phone settings at any time.

8. Children

The Game is intended for players aged 12 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Where a user is identified as being under the age of digital consent in their country (in Poland, 16), ad requests are marked accordingly and only non-personalised ads are shown. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Data sent from the Game is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to feedback and statistics data is limited to the developer.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the Game gets new features. The current version is always published at this address with its effective date. For significant changes we will also inform you in the Game.

11. Contact

Maria Kugacz (Rainy Evening Games), Poland — frameoneproduction@gmail.com
